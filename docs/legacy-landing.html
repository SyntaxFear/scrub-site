Everything it does

A real uninstall, not just to the Trash.

Dragging an app to the Trash leaves gigabytes of caches, containers and preferences behind. Scrub finds all of it and cleans up properly.

Complete uninstall

Removes the app plus every related file — Application Support, caches, preferences, containers, launch agents and system helpers.

Find old leftovers

A dedicated Leftovers tab hunts down files left behind by apps you already deleted — and skips Apple’s own system files.

True disk footprint

See exactly how much space each app really uses — the bundle and all of its scattered data — sorted biggest first.

One click, with a safety net

Your files go to the Trash, so it’s reversible. A built-in safety guard refuses to touch system-critical paths. Ever.

Reveal or remove anything

Inspect every item before it goes. Reveal it in Finder, or delete individual files — name-matched items stay unchecked until you confirm.

Quits, then cleans

If an app is still running, Scrub quits it gracefully first — then surfaces and removes its login items, launch agents and daemons.

Private by design

Your Mac, and nothing leaves it.

Everything stays local

Scrub scans your disk entirely on-device. There’s no account, no analytics, and no network calls — nothing about your Mac is ever sent anywhere.

Why it’s not on the App Store

To delete another app’s files and system-level helpers, Scrub needs deep disk access that the Mac App Store sandbox doesn’t allow. So it ships directly — signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
